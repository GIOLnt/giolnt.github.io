Text → Video

05

About

For thirty-five years I designed objects. Now I direct them.

I am an industrial designer and a 3D artist. I worked for fifteen years in Italy and for more than twenty in Greece, designing products and building them in 3D, from the first idea to the final render. Light, materials and proportion have always been my craft.

I started using artificial intelligence in my design work, connecting it to my 3D software to offer clients something better. Then a still image was no longer enough. I wanted to see my projects move, come alive, have a story.

For about two years I have been directing films with AI: historical documentaries for my YouTube channel TIMELESS GR, short films and commercials. The first results are coming in: with Antikythera we won third place in an international AI film competition, the MotionMux Wan 3.0 Hackathon.

I don’t work alone: every project is made together with Claude, Anthropic’s AI. I take care of the direction; together we write, build the prompts and edit. The method is still the design method: brief, idea, prototype, tests, final version.

I bring a designer’s eye; Claude brings speed and memory. Together we do what I have always done: take an idea and give it shape. Only now, it moves.
